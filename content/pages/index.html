Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a full renovation take?
Typically 8–14 weeks depending on scope. We provide a detailed timeline in our proposal and will confirm milestones during the pre-construction stage.
How much will my renovation cost?
Costs vary by scope and finishes. We offer a free site assessment and a detailed quote that breaks down materials, labour, and allowances for fixtures.
Do you provide warranties?
Yes — we provide workmanship warranties and facilitate manufacturer warranties for installed products. Specific warranty terms are included in your project contract.
Are you licensed and insured?
Our team includes licensed tradespeople and we maintain public liability insurance. We can provide certificates upon request during the proposal stage.
Do you handle swimming pool maintenance and repairs?
Yes — we offer pool construction, filter and pump repairs, maintenance, and chemical balancing. We provide service plans for ongoing pool upkeep.
What does your waterproofing service include?
Our waterproofing includes surface preparation, primer application, membrane installation, and post-works inspection. We provide a workmanship warranty for waterproofing works.
Can you install epoxy flooring and how durable is it?
Yes — our epoxy floor systems are suitable for residential and commercial areas. When properly applied, epoxy provides a durable, low-maintenance surface resistant to wear and chemicals.
Will electrical works comply with regulations?
All electrical installations are performed by certified electricians and comply with Singapore’s electrical safety standards. We can provide test certificates on completion.
How disruptive is a tile installation or full bathroom renovation?
We plan work in phases to minimise disruption, provide protection for your home, and maintain clear communication. Shorter jobs (e.g., tile replacement) can often be completed within days; full bathroom renovations take longer depending on scope.
How do I schedule a consultation or get a quote?
Call +65 8183 6772, WhatsApp us, or use the contact form to arrange a free site visit. We’ll follow up with an assessment and detailed proposal.
