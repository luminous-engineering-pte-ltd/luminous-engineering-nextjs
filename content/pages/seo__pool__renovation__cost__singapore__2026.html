Signs Your Pool Needs Renovation, Not Just Repair

  • Widespread tile cracking, discolouration, or missing grout across multiple sections
  • Persistent leaks that keep recurring despite spot repairs
  • Outdated or inefficient equipment (pumps, filters, heaters) constantly breaking down
  • Rough, faded, or peeling pool surface / plaster finish
  • Structural cracks in the pool shell or surrounding deck
  • You want to change the pool's design, add features (like a jacuzzi or water feature), or improve energy efficiency

If you're facing two or more of these issues simultaneously, a coordinated renovation is usually more cost-effective than a series of individual repairs spread over several years.

Common Pool Renovation Problems in Singapore

1. Tile and Surface Deterioration

Chlorine, sun exposure, and Singapore's heavy rainfall wear down tile grout, adhesive, and surface finishes over time, leading to cracked tiles, rough surfaces, and staining.

2. Ageing or Failing Waterproofing

The waterproofing membrane beneath tiles and plaster has a limited lifespan. Once it fails, water seeps into the structure, causing tile delamination and, eventually, leaks — see our related guide on pool leakage detection for more detail.

3. Outdated, Inefficient Equipment

Older pumps, filters, and heaters consume significantly more electricity than modern equivalents and are more prone to breakdown. Renovation is the ideal time to upgrade to energy-efficient systems.

4. Structural Cracks and Settlement

Ground movement, especially in landed properties, can cause hairline or structural cracks in the pool shell. Left untreated, these worsen over time and compromise the pool's integrity.

5. Outdated Design or Insufficient Features

Many homeowners renovate simply to modernise the look — new tile patterns, LED lighting, a deck upgrade, or the addition of a jacuzzi or water feature.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a full swimming pool renovation cost in Singapore?

A comprehensive renovation covering tiling, waterproofing, and equipment upgrades typically ranges from \$15,000 to \$45,000+, depending on pool size and scope. Request a free site assessment for an accurate quote.

Do I need to drain my pool for renovation?

Yes, most renovation work — especially re-tiling, resurfacing, and waterproofing repair — requires the pool to be fully drained.

Can I use my pool during renovation?

No, the pool will be out of use during structural, tiling, and waterproofing works for safety and to allow materials to cure properly.

Is it better to renovate or rebuild an old pool?

If the pool shell itself is structurally sound, renovation (re-tiling, waterproofing, equipment upgrade) is far more cost-effective than a full rebuild, which is typically only necessary for severe structural failure.

Do I need approval to renovate my pool in Singapore?

Requirements vary by property type and scope of work. Our licensed team advises on and manages any necessary permits as part of the renovation process.
