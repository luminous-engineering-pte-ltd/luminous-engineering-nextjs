What we coordinate

One accountable contractor for pool works

Swimming pool work often crosses multiple trades: structure, waterproofing, pumps, filters, valves, lighting, drainage and finishing. We help you define the scope, coordinate the right people and keep the project practical from site visit to handover.

Pool construction

New pool planning, build coordination, equipment planning and project scheduling for residential and commercial spaces.

Pool renovation

Tile, coping, waterproofing, resurfacing, plumbing and system upgrade coordination for older pools.

Equipment works

Pump, filter, heater, valve, chlorinator and automation replacement or repair planning.

Defect response

Leak checks, water loss investigation, pump-room review and practical repair recommendations.

Site and compliance review

Before work begins, the project scope can include checks of the following interfaces where applicable.

  • 01Pool shell, waterproofing and visible structural defects
  • 02Circulation, filtration, drainage and make-up water
  • 03Electrical isolation, bonding, lighting and equipment controls
  • 04Safe access, edge finishes and surrounding work areas
  • 05Water testing, commissioning and operator handover

FAQ

Licensed Swimming Pool Contractor FAQ

What pool contractor work do you handle?

We support pool construction, renovation, repair, equipment upgrades, leak rectification, maintenance planning and project coordination.

Can you coordinate electrical and plumbing interfaces?

Yes. Where the pool scope involves pumps, lighting, heaters, water supply, drainage or other interfaces, we coordinate the right specialist support.

How do I get a quote?

Call or WhatsApp +65 8183 6772 with photos, your location, the property type and what you need done. We will advise the next step.

Can you inspect an existing pool before quoting?

Yes. A site assessment helps us confirm visible defects, access, equipment condition and the practical scope before a quotation is finalised.

Can you renovate a pool without rebuilding everything?

Often, yes. Depending on the shell and systems, targeted waterproofing, resurfacing, fitting and equipment upgrades may be sufficient.

Do you provide support after handover?

Yes. We can advise on operation, maintenance planning and follow-up repair or equipment needs.
