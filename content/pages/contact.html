FREQUENTLY ASKED

Answers before we arrive.

If you need a signed NDA or customised invoicing workflow, we can accommodate it prior to kickoff.

How soon can you mobilise in Singapore?

+

Emergency teams are on standby 24/7 with average on-site arrival within 35 minutes. Planned works can start as early as 48 hours after site inspection, subject to permit approvals.

Do you help with MCST, URA, or BCA submissions?

+

Yes. Our compliance coordinators prepare method statements, structural calculations, and PE endorsements where required. We can also liaise directly with MCST and building management.

Are site visits really complimentary?

+

Absolutely. Initial walkthroughs, measurements, and feasibility reviews are fully complimentary within Singapore. Travel fees apply only for Sentosa/outer islands requiring special passes.

What payment structures do you support?

+

We offer milestone-based bank transfers, PayNow for smaller works, and escrow arrangements for large-scale renovations. Corporate clients can opt for 30-day terms upon approval.

Do you warranty your work?

+

Yes. Structural and waterproofing scopes carry up to 5-year warranties, while finishing and MEP scopes include 12-24 month coverage. Extended maintenance plans are available for estates and commercial assets.

Can you coordinate with overseas homeowners?

+

Many of our clients manage properties remotely. We provide secure digital sign-offs, regular photo/video reports, and PIN-protected access logs so you remain in control from anywhere in the world.
