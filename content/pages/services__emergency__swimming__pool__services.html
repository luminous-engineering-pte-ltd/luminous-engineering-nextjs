Emergency support

Pool problems that should not wait

A small pool fault can quickly become water damage, equipment damage, unsafe water or a facility closure. We help assess the urgency, stabilise what we can and plan the right repair path.

Water loss and leaks

Rapid water drop, damp areas, suspected pipe leaks or pool-shell leakage checks.

Pump and circulation failure

Noisy pumps, no flow, tripping equipment, blocked baskets or failed circulation.

Filter and equipment faults

Pressure problems, leaking valves, faulty heaters, chlorinators and automation issues.

Green or unsafe water

Algae outbreaks, cloudy water, chemical imbalance and urgent pool recovery advice.

What to do now

Before we arrive

1

Stay safe

If there is exposed wiring, burning smell, sparking, severe flooding or unsafe equipment, keep people away from the area and call us.

2

Send details

WhatsApp photos or videos of the pool, pump room, equipment labels, water level and the visible symptom.

3

Avoid guesswork

Do not keep restarting failing equipment repeatedly. It may worsen motor, electrical or plumbing damage.

Response workflow

From urgent call to repair plan

  • -We triage the issue by phone or WhatsApp so the right response is prioritised.
  • -We inspect the affected pool area, equipment room and visible plumbing or filtration symptoms.
  • -Where practical, we isolate or stabilise the immediate problem before planning the permanent fix.
  • -You get clear advice on repair, replacement, cleaning, water treatment or follow-up works.

Urgency guide

How we prioritise pool emergencies

Response and pricing depend on risk, fault type, access, parts and the work needed to make the pool stable.

PriorityTypical symptomsRecommended action
Immediate safetySparking, burning smell, exposed wiring or severe floodingKeep people away and call immediately
UrgentRapid water loss, pump trip, overflowing or no circulationStop repeated restarts and send photos
Same-day adviceGreen water, loud equipment, pressure or valve problemsAvoid swimming until assessed
Priority bookingMinor seepage, intermittent controls or declining clarityBook inspection before it worsens

Safety checklist

  • Keep swimmers and children away from unsafe water or equipment.
  • Do not touch wet electrical equipment or exposed wiring.
  • Do not repeatedly reset a tripping pump or control panel.
  • Use isolation controls only when they are safe and familiar to you.
  • Share your location and the clearest available photos or video.

What affects the response quote

  • Call-out time and property access
  • Diagnostic testing and fault isolation
  • Temporary stabilisation materials
  • Replacement parts and specialist trade support
  • Water treatment, cleaning and follow-up visits

FAQ

Emergency Pool Services FAQ

What pool emergencies do you handle?

We help with urgent leaks, pump and filter failures, overflowing pools, unsafe equipment concerns, green water and sudden circulation problems.

What should I send before your team comes?

Send photos or videos, your property type, location, equipment-room photos where safe and a short note about when the issue started.

Can you provide temporary stabilisation?

Where practical, yes. We can help isolate the issue, reduce immediate risk and advise the permanent repair or replacement plan.

Should anyone use the pool while there is a fault?

Keep swimmers out when water quality is uncertain, circulation has failed, the pool is overflowing or electrical equipment may be unsafe.

Can green pool water be recovered?

Often, yes. The recovery plan depends on testing, contamination level, circulation performance and filter condition.

Do you repair the underlying fault after stabilisation?

Yes. We can prepare the follow-up scope for repair, replacement, cleaning or water treatment after the immediate risk is controlled.
