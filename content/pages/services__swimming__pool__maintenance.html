Swimming Pool Maintenance in Singapore: The Luminous Engineering Guide

Luminous Comprehensive Pool Maintenance & Cleaning Services

At Luminous Engineering, we offer a comprehensive range of pool maintenance services tailored to meet the unique needs of your swimming pool. Our services include:

Routine Pool Cleaning

Our technicians will visit your property on a scheduled basis to perform a thorough cleaning and maintenance routine, including:

  • Skimming the surface to remove leaves, debris, and other contaminants
  • Brushing and vacuuming the pool walls, floor, and steps
  • Backwashing and cleaning filters
  • Inspecting and maintaining pool equipment to extend their longevity (pumps, filters, heaters, etc.)

We are able to accommodate customisations for your package, speak with us to learn more.

Water Treatment and Sanitisation

Maintaining proper water chemistry is crucial for a safe and enjoyable swimming experience. Our professionals use advanced water testing methods to ensure your pool water meets the necessary chemical balance to prevent algae growth.

We offer a range of sanitisation solutions, including salt chlorination systems and chemical treatment.

Pool Equipment Repair and Replacement

From pumps and filters to heaters and lighting systems, we have the expertise to repair, maintain, and replace all types of pool equipment. Our technicians use only high-quality, durable parts and components to ensure optimal performance and longevity.

Pool Inspections and Leak Detection

Regular inspections and leak detection services help identify potential issues before they escalate into costly repairs. Our team uses state-of-the-art equipment to locate and resolve leaks promptly, minimising water loss and structural damage.

Pool Resurfacing and Renovation

Over time, pool surfaces can become worn, stained, or damaged, affecting the overall appearance and functionality of your pool. Our team can expertly handle pool resurfacing projects, including re-plastering, tile replacement, and deck renovations, breathing new life into your outdoor oasis.

The Importance of Regular Pool Maintenance

A well-maintained swimming pool not only enhances its aesthetic appeal but also ensures the water quality meets the necessary health and safety standards set by the National Environment Agency (NEA) in Singapore. Neglecting regular maintenance can lead to a host of issues, including:

  • Algae growth
  • Cloudy or discoloured water
  • Accumulation of debris and contaminants
  • Imbalanced water chemistry
  • Equipment malfunction or damage

These problems can pose health risks to swimmers and lead to costly repairs down the line. By investing in professional pool maintenance services, you can protect your investment and create a safe, inviting environment for your family and guests to enjoy. FAQs About Swimming Pool Maintenance in Singapore

How often should I have my pool maintained?

Regular maintenance is recommended at least once a week to ensure water quality and equipment functionality.

Can I perform pool maintenance myself?

While basic skimming can be done yourself, professional maintenance ensures proper chemical balancing and equipment checks that are crucial for safety.

What are the signs that my pool needs professional maintenance?

Cloudy water, algae growth, strong chlorine smell, or equipment making strange noises are all signs you need professional help.

How long does a typical pool maintenance visit take?

A standard maintenance visit usually takes between 45 minutes to an hour, depending on the pool size and condition.

Yes, we offer 24/7 emergency services for urgent pool issues.
