Comprehensive Swimming Pool Services We Offer

With a decade of experience and hundreds of satisfied customers, we provide complete swimming pool service, including pool maintenance, pool repair, pool construction, and water feature installations.

1. Pool Cleaning & Maintenance

A clean pool isn't just about aesthetics—it's about safety, health, and long-term cost savings. Many pool owners face issues like:

  • Cloudy or green water – caused by algae or improper chemical balance
  • Clogged filters or pumps – leading to higher electricity bills and inefficient circulation
  • Hidden leaks or cracks – small problems that can quickly become expensive repairs
  • Debris buildup – leaves, dirt, and contaminants that spoil your pool experience

Our Solutions:

  • Regular debris removal and skimming
  • Chemical balancing and algae prevention
  • Filter cleaning and pump inspection
  • Full inspections to detect leaks or structural issues early

With our tailored swimming pool maintenance services, your pool stays sparkling, safe, and ready for use anytime.

Pool Maintenance Packages – Keep Your Pool Sparkling Year-Round

A swimming pool is more than just a luxury—it's an investment in your home and lifestyle. But keeping it clean, safe, and enjoyable requires regular care. At Luminous Engineering PTE Ltd., we provide structured pool maintenance packages in Singapore that prevent costly repairs, improve water quality, and ensure your pool is always ready for a swim.

📅

1. Weekly Maintenance

Our weekly maintenance keeps your pool looking pristine and prevents small problems from turning into big headaches. We:

  • Skim leaves and debris to keep water clear
  • Brush surfaces to avoid algae buildup
  • Check water chemistry for safe swimming

With consistent weekly care, your pool stays healthy, inviting, and ready for use every day.

🔄

2. Bi-Weekly Maintenance

For pools that need more thorough attention, our bi-weekly service dives a little deeper:

  • Vacuuming to remove settled dirt
  • Balancing pH, chlorine, and other chemicals
  • Inspecting filtration systems for smooth operation

Bi-weekly maintenance ensures water stays crystal-clear and equipment runs efficiently, saving you time and long-term repair costs.

📋

3. Monthly Maintenance

Monthly checks focus on keeping your pool systems in top condition:

  • Inspecting pumps, filters, and circulation systems
  • Identifying wear or potential issues before they become costly problems
  • Optimizing overall pool performance

These monthly inspections complement your regular cleaning schedule and give peace of mind that everything is running smoothly.

✨

4. One-Time Deep Cleaning

Sometimes your pool needs a reset. Our one-time deep cleaning tackles:

  • Stubborn stains and algae buildup
  • Hard-to-reach corners and surfaces
  • Refreshing water clarity and circulation

This service gives your pool a fresh start, boosting your regular maintenance routine and restoring a sparkling, inviting appearance.

🚨

5. Emergency Pool Recovery (Green or Cloudy Water)

When your pool turns green or cloudy, it's more than an eyesore—it can be a health risk. Our emergency recovery service:

  • Corrects chemical imbalances quickly
  • Removes algae and contaminants
  • Restores clear, safe, swim-ready water

This service is perfect for urgent situations and ensures your pool stays reliable and safe throughout the year.

FAQs About Swimming Pool Contractors in Singapore

What types of swimming pools can you build?

We specialise in building various types of pools including concrete pools, fiberglass pools, vinyl liner pools, infinity pools, and skimmer pools, tailored to your specific needs and space.

Do you provide pool maintenance services after construction?

Yes, we offer comprehensive pool maintenance packages to ensure your pool remains clean, safe, and in optimal condition long after construction is complete.

How long does it take to construct a swimming pool?

The construction timeline varies depending on the pool type and complexity, but typically ranges from 8 to 12 weeks for concrete pools.

What are the average costs for building a pool in Singapore?

Costs vary significantly based on size, materials, and features. We recommend contacting us for a personalised, no-obligation quote to get an accurate estimate.

Do you handle pool renovations or upgrades?

Absolutely. We specialise in pool renovations, resurfacing, equipment upgrades, and aesthetic enhancements to breathe new life into your existing pool.

Are your designs eco-friendly?

Yes, we incorporate eco-friendly practices such as energy-efficient pumps, LED lighting, and water-saving filtration systems to minimise environmental impact.

Do you assist with permits and regulations?

Yes, we handle all necessary submissions and approvals with relevant authorities to ensure your pool construction complies with Singapore's regulations.

What is included in the warranty for new pools?

Our new pools come with a structural warranty and warranties on equipment and workmanship. Specific terms will be detailed in your contract.

What ongoing costs should I expect?

Ongoing costs include chemicals, electricity for pumps/lights, water, and regular maintenance services. We can provide an estimated monthly breakdown.

Can you design pools for small spaces or rooftops?

Yes, we are experts in maximising space and can design plunge pools, lap pools, or rooftop pools that fit perfectly within limited areas.
