Complete Swimming Pool Repair Services in Birch Road Singapore

At Luminous Engineering, we handle every type of swimming pool problem with skill and care. Our services help keep your pool clean, safe, and fully functional.

Leak Detection and Repair

Water leaks are one of the most common issues pool owners face. A leaking pool wastes water and can harm the pool's structure. If you notice water levels lowering faster than normal, it may be time for a leak check. Our team uses professional tools to find the exact source of leaks. After we locate the problem, we fix it with strong, long-lasting materials so your pool stops losing water and stays protected.

Pump and Motor Repair

The pump and motor are very important for keeping pool water clean and moving. When the pump fails or works weakly, water circulation slows down and makes the pool dirty. We check all parts of the pump system, including the motor, seals, and connections. We repair or replace any parts that are worn or broken. After our service, your water flows smoothly again and stays cleaner longer.

Filtration System Services

A good filtration system traps dirt and keeps pool water clear. When filters become blocked, dirty, or damaged, water quality gets worse and algae can grow. We inspect sand filters, cartridge filters, and other filtration equipment. We fix or replace parts so your filtration works properly and keeps water clean and safe for swimming.

Tile and Surface Repair

Tiles that are chipped, loose, or damaged not only look bad, they can also hurt feet and allow water to damage the pool structure under the surface. Our team replaces damaged tiles and repairs worn surfaces with care. We make your pool look neat, safe, and attractive again. Smooth finishes also help prevent injury and make cleaning easier.

Structural Repair and Waterproofing

Some problems run deeper than the surface. Cracks in the pool shell or worn down waterproofing can weaken the structure of the pool and cause serious damage over time. We fix cracks in concrete, restore the strength of the pool shell, and apply waterproofing materials where needed. This protects your pool from water stress and ensures it stays stable for years.

Water Testing and Chemical Balancing

Clean water is safe water. Unbalanced chemicals can cause skin irritation, eye redness, and an unpleasant smell. We test your pool water and adjust chlorine levels, pH balance, and other important chemical measures. Balanced water protects swimmers and helps your filtration system work better.

Emergency Pool Repair Service

Pool problems can happen unexpectedly. When you need help quickly, we respond fast and provide urgent repair solutions so you don't have to wait and worry.

Signs Your Pool Needs Professional Repair

  • Water level dropping fast
  • Pump making unusual sounds
  • Water looks cloudy, green, or unhealthy
  • Tiles are cracked, loose, or missing
  • Slow water circulation
  • Chemical balance problems (skin irritation or smell)
  • Visible cracks in the pool surface

If you notice any of these issues, it means your pool needs professional attention. Acting early helps prevent bigger, more costly problems later.
