EPOXY FLOOR COATING SERVICES IN SINGAPORE

Luminous Engineering provides professional epoxy floor coating services across Singapore for residential, commercial, and industrial properties. Our expert team transforms ordinary concrete floors into durable, attractive, and easy-to-maintain surfaces.

With over 10 years of experience in flooring solutions, we use premium-grade epoxy materials and advanced application techniques to ensure a flawless, long-lasting finish. Whether you need epoxy flooring for your home garage, warehouse, factory, retail space, or commercial kitchen, we have the expertise to deliver outstanding results.

Our epoxy flooring systems are designed to withstand Singapore's tropical climate, providing superior protection against moisture, chemicals, abrasion, and heavy traffic. We offer a wide range of colors, finishes, and decorative options to match your aesthetic preferences while delivering unmatched durability.

Luminous Engineering stands behind every installation with comprehensive warranties and post-installation support. We're committed to providing transparent pricing, timely project completion, and customer satisfaction that keeps clients returning for all their flooring needs.

1. Residential Epoxy Flooring

Transform your home's garage, basement, or living spaces with professional epoxy floor coatings that combine beauty with durability.

Our residential epoxy solutions are perfect for homeowners looking to upgrade their concrete floors. We offer a variety of colors and finishes including solid colors, decorative flakes, and metallic effects that complement your home's design.

The epoxy coating protects against stains, moisture damage, and wear from daily use. It's easy to clean with just soap and water, making it ideal for busy households.

2. Commercial Epoxy Flooring

For retail stores, showrooms, restaurants, and offices, our commercial epoxy flooring provides a professional appearance with exceptional durability.

High foot traffic is no problem for our commercial-grade epoxy systems. The seamless, non-porous surface resists spills and stains while creating a bright, welcoming environment for customers and employees. Available in multiple finish options from high-gloss to matte, our coatings enhance your brand image while reducing long-term maintenance costs.

3. Industrial & Warehouse Flooring

Heavy-duty epoxy coatings engineered for the toughest environments. Our industrial flooring systems withstand forklift traffic, chemical exposure, and extreme conditions.

We use specialized formulations that provide superior impact resistance and chemical protection. Safety features like anti-slip additives can be incorporated to meet workplace safety requirements. Our industrial epoxy floors are designed for decades of service with minimal maintenance, even in the most demanding manufacturing and logistics facilities.

4. Garage Epoxy Coating

Turn your garage into a showroom-quality space with our specialized garage epoxy coatings. Resistant to oil, gasoline, and automotive fluids.

Our garage epoxy systems are specifically formulated to handle hot tire pickup and thermal shock from vehicle tires. The high-gloss finish brightens your garage while the durable coating protects the concrete from cracking and pitting. Choose from solid colors or add decorative flakes for a custom look that increases your property value.

5. Epoxy Floor Repair & Refinishing

Existing epoxy floor showing signs of wear? We provide expert repair and refinishing services to restore your floor's appearance and protective properties.

Our technicians can repair damaged areas, remove old coatings, and apply fresh epoxy to give your floor a complete makeover. We address issues like peeling, discoloration, and surface damage to extend the life of your flooring investment. Regular maintenance and timely repairs ensure your epoxy floor continues to look great and perform well for years to come.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions about Epoxy Flooring in Singapore

How long does epoxy flooring last?

With proper installation and maintenance, epoxy flooring can last 15-20 years or even longer. The lifespan depends on factors like foot traffic, usage, and maintenance. Our premium systems come with warranties up to 15 years, and we've seen many floors remain in excellent condition for decades.

How long does the installation take?

Most residential projects are completed within 2-3 days, including surface preparation, coating application, and curing time. The floor is ready for light foot traffic after 24 hours and full vehicle/heavy traffic after 48-72 hours. Larger commercial or industrial projects may take longer depending on the area size and complexity.

Can epoxy be applied over existing floors?

Yes, epoxy can be applied over existing concrete floors that are in good condition. It can also be applied over certain tiles after proper preparation. However, the surface must be structurally sound, clean, and free from moisture problems. Our technicians will conduct a thorough assessment during the free consultation.

Is epoxy flooring slippery when wet?
  • High-gloss epoxy can be slippery when wet
  • We offer anti-slip additives for enhanced traction
  • Decorative flakes provide natural slip resistance
  • Textured topcoats available for safety-critical areas
  • We'll recommend the best solution based on your needs
How do I maintain epoxy floors?

Epoxy floors are incredibly low-maintenance. Regular sweeping or dust mopping and occasional cleaning with a mild detergent and water is sufficient. Avoid harsh chemicals, abrasive cleaners, and steel wool. For high-traffic commercial areas, periodic professional cleaning helps maintain the shine and extends the floor's life.

What colors and finishes are available?

We offer an extensive range of options including solid colors, metallic effects, decorative color flakes, and custom designs. Finish options include high-gloss, semi-gloss, satin, and matte. During your consultation, we'll show you samples and help you choose the perfect combination to match your space and preferences.

Is epoxy suitable for Singapore's climate?

Absolutely. Our epoxy systems are specifically formulated for tropical climates like Singapore. They resist high humidity, temperature fluctuations, and UV exposure. We use moisture-resistant primers and UV-stable topcoats to ensure your floor maintains its beauty and structural integrity for years to come.

Do you offer warranties on epoxy installations?

Yes, all installations come with comprehensive warranties ranging from 5 to 15 years depending on the system and package you choose. We stand behind our work and use only premium materials from trusted international suppliers. Our warranty covers issues like delamination, peeling, and manufacturing defects.
