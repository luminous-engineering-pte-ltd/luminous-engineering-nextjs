How to Choose the Best HDB Electrician or Licensed Electrician in Singapore

Selecting the right electrician is essential for ensuring the safety, compliance, and longevity of your electrical installations. Here are the key factors to consider:

  • Verify EMA Licence: Always confirm your electrician holds a valid licence issued by the Energy Market Authority. This is a legal requirement for anyone carrying out electrical work in Singapore — an unlicensed electrician may perform substandard work that puts your property and safety at risk.
  • Check for Insurance Coverage: A reputable provider of electrical services Singapore-wide should carry public liability insurance and workmen's compensation, protecting you from financial liability in the event of an accident.
  • Ask for a Detailed Quotation: Request a comprehensive, itemised quote covering scope of work, materials, labour and any additional fees. Avoid electricians who provide vague or verbal-only quotes.
  • Read Customer Reviews: Check Google reviews and other platforms for genuine feedback. Consistent praise for punctuality, quality and professionalism is a good sign.
  • Match the Electrician to Your Property Type: Some contractors specialise in residential work, others in commercial fit-outs. If you live in public housing, look specifically for a best HDB electrician Singapore has to offer; businesses should look for a dedicated commercial electrician instead.
  • Compare at Least 3 Quotes: Don't settle for the first quote — compare pricing, scope and terms before deciding. The cheapest option isn't always the best value.
  • Ask About Warranty on Workmanship: A confident, competent electrician will stand behind their work with a warranty, giving you recourse if issues arise after the job is completed.
