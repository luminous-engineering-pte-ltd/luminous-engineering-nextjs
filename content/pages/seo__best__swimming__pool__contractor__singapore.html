How to Choose a Pool Contractor

  • Ask for a written scope and quotation.
  • Check whether diagnosis is included before major repair.
  • Confirm warranty terms and exclusions in writing.
  • Review experience with the specific pool problem you have.
  • Avoid choosing on the lowest price alone when waterproofing or structural work is involved.
