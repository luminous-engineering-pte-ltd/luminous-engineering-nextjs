Our Waterproofing Repair Process

A certain repair process to make sure you get the finest outcome and get rid of seepage is what we do. Every step is carefully planned.

1

Locating The Leak Source

We start by identifying where the water is coming from. Next, our team uses the latest tools and precise methods to help locate the exact origin of leaks or seepage.

2

Mending Holes

Our team then fixes any cracks, holes, or damage in walls, floors, or ceilings to stop water from getting in. Besides, we ensure to smooth out the surface.

3

Surface Cleaning

Cleaning the surface to prepare it for the upcoming waterproofing is what we do in this step. Then, our team removes the grime or dirt using the right methods.

4

Use Proper Waterproofing

We use the right waterproofing solutions that keep the surface dry, clean, and protected from seepage. Plus, every layer is applied evenly for a strong and lasting finish.

5

Patch Gaps & Leaks

Our team closes off any gaps, joints, or seams with high-quality waterproofing compounds. That way it also creates a strong and watertight seal.

6

Final Waterproofing Report

Lastly, we wrap up with a detailed report showing the work completed. Plus, our team has full transparency to give you peace of mind and know how we work to get the results.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions about Waterproofing Services in Singapore

How long does waterproofing last?

Quality waterproofing can last 10-15 years or more depending on the materials used, application method, and environmental conditions. We provide warranty coverage for our waterproofing work.

What are signs that my property needs waterproofing?
  • Water stains on walls or ceilings
  • Peeling paint or wallpaper
  • Musty odors indicating mold
  • Visible cracks in walls or floors
  • Damp or wet patches
How long does a waterproofing project take?

A typical bathroom waterproofing takes 2-3 days. Larger projects like roof or external wall waterproofing may take 5-7 days depending on the size and complexity. We'll provide a detailed timeline during consultation.

Do you provide warranty for waterproofing work?

Yes, we provide a comprehensive warranty for all waterproofing services. The warranty period varies depending on the service type and materials used, typically ranging from 5-10 years.

What waterproofing materials do you use?

We use high-quality waterproofing materials from trusted brands including cementitious coatings, liquid membranes, polyurethane coatings, and EPDM rubber membranes. The choice depends on the specific application and requirements.
