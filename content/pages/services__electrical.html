FAQ

Electrical Services FAQ

Do all service cards have dedicated pages?
Yes. Every electrical service listed on this page now links to its own dedicated Luminous Engineering page.
Do you provide emergency electrical support?
Yes. Use the emergency electrician page or WhatsApp us if there are sparks, burning smells, power failure or breakers that will not reset.
Can you help with old wiring and DB boxes?
Yes. We inspect the existing wiring and DB condition before recommending repair, partial rewiring, full rewiring or DB upgrade.
Do you handle non-traditional electrical services such as CCTV or smart home wiring?
Yes. The electrical services section includes connected systems such as CCTV, intercoms, smart switches, EV chargers and network cabling.
How do I get a quote for Electrical Services Singapore?
Call or WhatsApp +65 8183 6772 with photos, property type, location and preferred timing. We will advise the next step and quote where possible.
How soon can Luminous Engineering start electrical services singapore work?
Start dates depend on current bookings, scope and access requirements. Urgent issues are prioritised where practical.
Do you provide a clear price before work begins?
Yes. We explain the scope, pricing and any assumptions before starting. Any additional work is discussed before it proceeds.
Which areas in Singapore do you serve?
We support customers island-wide across Singapore, subject to appointment availability and project requirements.
Can you work with HDB, condo, landed and commercial properties?
Yes. We regularly support residential and commercial properties, with planning adjusted for each property type.
Do I need to be on site during the work?
It is helpful for assessment and handover, but arrangements can be discussed if access can be provided safely.
