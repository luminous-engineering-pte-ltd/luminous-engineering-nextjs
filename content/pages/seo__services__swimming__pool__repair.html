Our Repair Process

  1. Triage the issue by phone or WhatsApp.
  2. Review photos, videos and symptoms.
  3. Inspect the pool, plant room and equipment.
  4. Identify likely fault sources.
  5. Explain repair or replacement options.
  6. Confirm scope and quotation.
  7. Carry out repair or installation work.
  8. Test the system and advise maintenance steps.

Repair vs Replace

We recommend repair when the fault is isolated and parts are practical. Replacement may be better for old, inefficient, repeatedly failing or unsafe equipment.
