Introduction

Plumbing is the backbone of every home and commercial property in Singapore. From the moment you turn on a tap to the flush of a toilet, a reliable plumbing system ensures your daily routines run smoothly. Yet most people only think about plumbing when something goes wrong — a burst pipe flooding the kitchen at midnight, a stubbornly choked toilet, or a persistent drip that quietly drives up your PUB water bill. When these issues strike, having a dependable plumbing company on speed dial can save you from costly water damage, structural deterioration, and hours of frustration.

Singapore's high-rise living environment and ageing HDB infrastructure mean plumbing problems are remarkably common. Corroded galvanised iron pipes in older flats, concealed leaks behind bathroom tiles, and faulty water heater connections are everyday issues that licensed plumbers deal with across the island. Whether you need a simple tap replacement, a full bathroom re-piping, or 24/7 emergency leak repair, the quality of the plumber you engage makes all the difference between a lasting fix and a recurring headache.

To help you make an informed decision, we've researched and evaluated dozens of plumbing service providers and compiled this comprehensive guide to the 10 best plumbing companies in Singapore for 2026. Each company has been assessed on service range, response time, pricing transparency, customer feedback, and overall reliability. Let's dive in.

How to Choose the Right Plumbing Company in Singapore

With so many plumbing companies operating in Singapore, narrowing down the right one for your needs can feel overwhelming. Here are the key factors to consider when evaluating potential plumbers for your home or commercial property:

  • Check for PUB licensing and registration — In Singapore, plumbing work that involves the public water supply system must be carried out by a PUB-licensed plumber. Always verify that the company employs licensed plumbers to ensure compliance with regulations and the safety of your water system.
  • Request an on-site inspection before accepting a quotation — A reputable plumber should inspect the actual problem before giving you a price. Be cautious of companies that quote fixed prices over the phone without seeing the issue, as the final bill may end up much higher once they discover the true scope of the work.
  • Read online reviews and check ratings — Google Reviews, Facebook reviews, and forums like HardwareZone provide valuable insights from real customers. Look for consistent patterns — repeated praise for punctuality, quality, and fair pricing is a strong positive indicator.
  • Compare at least three quotations — Getting multiple quotes helps you understand the fair market rate for your specific plumbing job. Compare not just the bottom-line price, but also the scope of work, materials specified, warranty terms, and estimated completion time.
  • Ask about warranty and after-service support — Professional plumbing companies should provide a workmanship warranty for their repairs and installations. Clarify the warranty duration, what it covers, and the process for making a claim if issues arise after the job is completed.
  • Evaluate response time and availability — Plumbing emergencies don't follow a schedule. If 24/7 availability is important to you, confirm that the company offers genuine round-the-clock emergency service and ask about their average response time for urgent calls.
  • Confirm pricing transparency — A trustworthy plumber provides a clear, itemised quotation that breaks down labour charges, material costs, and any additional fees. Avoid companies that give vague estimates or refuse to put their pricing in writing.

Taking the time to properly vet your plumbing company upfront can save you from substandard work, unexpected costs, and the hassle of having to engage a second plumber to fix what the first one got wrong.
